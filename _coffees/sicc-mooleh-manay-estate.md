Light roast carbonic maceration lot from Mooleh Manay Estate in North Coorg, harvested and processed in collaboration with South India Coffee Company (SICC). Whole cherries are sealed in oxygen-free tanks to develop a silky, juicy cup with a floral, wine-like aroma, bright plum and red cherry sweetness, and a lively orange peel finish.
